What is Kafka?
Kafka is a distributed event streaming platform used for:
- asynchronous communication
- event-driven architecture
- high-throughput data processing
- decoupling microservices
- What is a Topic?
 A topic is a logical category where Kafka stores messages/events.
- What is a Partition?
 A topic is divided into partitions.
- What is an Offset?
  Every record in a partition gets an offset.Offset identifies the position of a record within that partition.
- Producer vs Consumer
  Producer: Publishes records to Kafka.
- Consumer: Reads records from Kafka.
- . What is Consumer Group?
  Consumers within the same consumer group share the work.
But another consumer group can independently read the same messages:
Within one consumer group: one partition is assigned to at most one consumer at a time.

- Does Kafka guarantee message ordering?
  Kafka guarantees ordering within a partition.
  Kafka guarantees ordering within a partition, not across multiple partitions."
