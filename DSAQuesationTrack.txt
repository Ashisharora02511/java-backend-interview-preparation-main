REVISION RULE
==================================================

1. Revise a question if you needed a hint or made a logical mistake.
2. Re-solve important questions without looking at old code.
3. Explain the approach aloud as if answering an interviewer.
4. Practise edge cases and complexity analysis.
5. Move to Medium only after the Easy pattern is understood.
6. Advanced problems are selective, not mandatory for every topic.
